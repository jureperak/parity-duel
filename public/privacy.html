Privacy

Even or Odd is a small game for Microsoft Teams meetings. This page explains what it handles.

What the game uses

  • Your Teams display name, so other players see who is playing.
  • Your profile photo (48×48 thumbnail), only if the app has been set up for photos and you agree to share it.
  • The numbers you pick and which side you play.

Where it goes

This data is shared only with people in the same meeting, through Microsoft Teams Live Share. The game has no server of its own and the developer does not collect, store or see it. It disappears with the meeting session.

Third parties

  • The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data such as IP addresses (see GitHub’s privacy statement).
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • Microsoft processes meeting data under your organization’s Microsoft 365 terms.
